## Team and mission

The AD Division role designs, implements, and optimizes core autonomous-driving controllers and motion-planning algorithms. The work combines control-theory fundamentals and vehicle-dynamics knowledge to achieve robust and dependable behavior on real vehicles.

The engineer collaborates with planning, AI, and systems-engineering teams to connect learning-based policies with conventional model-based control and contribute to Physical-AI-based autonomous-driving systems.

## Responsibilities

- Design, simulate, implement, and deploy controllers based on methods such as MPC, LQR/LQG, sliding-mode control, and PID.
- Formulate and solve real-time trajectory-optimization problems with nonlinear physical constraints.
- Build deterministic, real-time, safety-oriented software in C++ and ROS2.
- Integrate planning outputs, RL/IL policies, and filtering algorithms into the control architecture.
- Validate control performance and tune parameters on real vehicle hardware.
- Improve vehicle stability, ride comfort, and safety through control-system optimization.

## Required qualifications

- Master's degree or higher in control, electrical, mechanical, automotive, robotics, or a related engineering field, plus at least three years of relevant professional experience, or equivalent experience.
- Strong understanding of classical and modern control methods, including linear and nonlinear system modeling.
- Embedded and real-time C++ software-development experience.
- Experience with ROS2 or a comparable middleware environment.
- Demonstrated automotive or robotics experience controlling and tuning physical actuators through production-level deployment.

## Preferred qualifications

- PhD in control, robotics, or a related discipline.
- Practical use of numerical-optimization tools such as CasADi, OSQP, or Ipopt.
- Experience formulating and implementing real-time QP or NLP problems.
- Strong understanding of production-grade motion planning and state-estimation filters.
- Collaboration with or integration of reinforcement-learning or imitation-learning control policies.
- Experience combining generative AI or learned policies with conventional control in safety-critical applications.
- Publications in leading control or robotics venues such as CDC, ACC, ICRA, or IROS.
- Understanding of automotive/robotics safety and coding standards such as ISO 26262 and MISRA C++.

## Interview preparation anchors

- MPC/MPPI formulation, constraints, cost design, solver or sampling tradeoffs, and real-time feasibility.
- Vehicle-dynamics modeling and the boundary between planning and low-level control.
- Real-vehicle integration involving actuators, communication, timing, calibration, and validation.
- C++ implementation and debugging for deterministic real-time behavior.
- ROS2 or comparable middleware concepts without overstating unverified hands-on experience.
- How a learned RL/IL policy can be combined with filters, guardrails, and model-based control.
- Quantitative treatment of stability, comfort, safety, and failure handling.
