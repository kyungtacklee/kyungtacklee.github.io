## Team and mission

The position is part of NVIDIA's Solutions Engineering work for autonomous vehicles. Its purpose is to help shape and deploy scalable autonomous-driving solutions, with particular responsibility for application architecture, planning and control integration, vehicle drivability, system analysis, and partner-facing deployment.

The role works across NVIDIA internal architecture and software teams as well as external vehicle-platform partners. It is therefore a combination of planning/control engineering, vehicle integration, performance analysis, technical troubleshooting, and global collaboration.

## Responsibilities

- Influence NVIDIA's internal autonomous-driving application architecture from the planning-and-control perspective.
- Integrate NVIDIA planning and control solutions into target vehicles.
- Adapt and tune planners and controllers to satisfy vehicle-dynamics performance targets and functional requirements.
- Lead system and vehicle bring-up and resolve planning, control, and functional integration issues.
- Use simulation and real-vehicle test drives, including recorded data, for functional and performance analysis.
- Integrate hardware and software components with partners and understand the complete system architecture.
- Profile performance, identify bottlenecks, and drive system optimization.
- Collaborate with engineering teams across the United States, APAC, and Europe for customer deployments.

## Required qualifications

- At least five years of relevant software-development experience in autonomous driving and/or deep-learning-related technology.
- Strong fundamentals in control theory, motion planning, and optimization.
- Understanding of real-time algorithms for safety-critical systems.
- Strong knowledge of vehicle dynamics, state estimation, and trajectory generation.
- Experience transferring algorithms from simulation to real-time hardware.
- BS or MS in computer science, robotics, electrical engineering, a related technical discipline, or equivalent experience.
- Strong C/C++ development skills and working knowledge of Python.
- Ability to work in fast development cycles and cross-functional organizations.
- Strong analytical problem solving and attention to detail.

## Differentiating qualifications

- Familiarity with NVIDIA DRIVE, NVIDIA GPU hardware, and CUDA.
- QNX or another real-time operating system.
- Automotive engineering processes and standards, including ISO 26262, ISO 21448/SOTIF, and ASPICE.
- In-vehicle testing and simulation tied to measurable engineering outcomes.
- Proven planning-and-control development across multiple vehicle platforms.
- Open-source ownership or contribution and mentoring experience.

## Interview preparation anchors

- Explain one planning/control function from architecture through simulation, real-time implementation, vehicle integration, calibration, and validation.
- Prepare examples of planner/controller adaptation for differences in vehicle dynamics and actuator behavior.
- Be ready to diagnose a system-level drivability or performance issue using logged data.
- Demonstrate collaboration across control, software, hardware, validation, and customer teams.
- Clearly distinguish verified experience from technologies that are only listed as preferred qualifications.
- Prepare the technical discussion in English because the role collaborates with global teams.
