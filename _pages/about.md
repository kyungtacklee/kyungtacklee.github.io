My overarching research aims to design systems, control, and decision-making algorithms to develop safe, robust, and efficient dynamical systems. In particular, my research is based on multi-body dynamics analysis, control theory, optimization, mathematical analysis, game theory, machine learning, and robotics.

Kyungtack Lee received the B.S. degree in mechanical engineering from Ajou University, South Korea, in 2012, and the M.S degree in mechanical engineering from the Seoul National University, South Korea, in 2022, where he is pursuing a Ph.D. in mechanical engineering. From 2012 to 2015, he worked as a Research Engineer at Samsung Techwin R&D Center (Now Hanwha Aerospace) in South Korea. Since 2016, he has been with HL Mando Corporation, South Korea, where he is currently a Senior Research Engineer of the Mobility Motion Control Team. His research interests include dynamic system design and analysis, vehicle motion control, and autonomous driving.
