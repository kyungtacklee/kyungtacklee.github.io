## E-Corner Module System Design

**Role:** System design and analysis<br>
**Organization:** HL Mando<br>
**Period:** 2019–2020

I worked on the system design of an E-Corner module that brings propulsion, steering, braking, and suspension functions together at the wheel. The work required coordinating mechanical packaging with electrical and control interfaces, while keeping the module useful for four-wheel independent steering and driving concepts.

This project connects my earlier mechanical-system background with later work in 4WISD vehicle control. Related wheel-module inventions progressed to registered Korean patents, with additional electric-drive and suspension inventions documented in the patent portfolio.

**Key areas:** E-Corner, wheel modules, system architecture, interface design, 4WISD
